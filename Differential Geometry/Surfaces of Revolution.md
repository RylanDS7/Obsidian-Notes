A surface of revolution is defined to be the set $S \in \mathbb{R}^3$ given by rotating a connected [[Regularly Parameterized Curve]] (plane curve), $C$ about an axis in the plane which does not meet the curve. Taking the $xz$ plane as the plane of the curve, we can parameterize
$$
x = f(v) \hspace{20pt} y = g(v) \hspace{20pt} a < v < b \hspace{20pt} f(v) > 0
$$
and denote the rotation angle about the $z$ axis by $u$. This leads to the map for the surface $S$ given by
$$
\mathbf{x}(u,v) = \big(f(v)\cos u, f(v)\sin u, g(v)\big)
$$
which defines a [[Regular Surfaces]].
