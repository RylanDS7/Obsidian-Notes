The tangent plane of a [[Regular Surfaces]] $S$ at $p$ is denoted $T_p(S)$. Vectors in the tangent plane are tangent to $S$ at $p$.

Let $\mathbf{x} : U \subset \mathbb{R}^2 \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^3$ be a parameterization of the regular surface $S$ and let $q \in U$. The vector subspace of dimension 2 defined by the [[Differential]],
$$
d\mathbf{x}_q(\mathbb{R}^2) \subset \mathbb{R}^3
$$
coincides with the set of tangent vectors to $S$ at $\mathbf{x}(q)$. That is, $d\mathbf{x}_q(\mathbb{R}^2) = T_{\mathbf{x}(q)}(S)$.
