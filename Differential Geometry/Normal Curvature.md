For a [[Regularly Parameterized Curve]] $C$ in a [[Regular Surfaces]] S passing through $p \in S$, where $k$ is the curvature ([[Curvature and Torsion]]) of $C$ at $p$ and $\cos (\theta) = \langle n, N\rangle$ where $n$ is the normal vector to $C$ and $N$ is the normal vector to $S$ (given by the [[Gauss Map]]) at $p$. The number
$$
k_n = k\cos(\theta)
$$
is the normal curvature of $C \subset S$ at $p$.

This is basically the projection of the curvature of $C$ onto the normal of the surface.
