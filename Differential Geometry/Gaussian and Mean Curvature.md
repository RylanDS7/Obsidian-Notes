For a surface $S$ with $p \in S$ take the [[Differential]] of the [[Gauss Map]] given by $dN_p : T_p(S) \rightarrow T_p(S)$. The determinant of $dN_p$ is the Gaussian curvature $K$ of $S$ at $p$ and negative of half the trace of $dN_p$ is the mean curvature $H$ of $S$ at $p$. In terms of the [[Principal Curvatures]] these are given by
$$
K = k_1k_2 \hspace{30pt} H = \frac{k_1 + k_2}{2}
$$
