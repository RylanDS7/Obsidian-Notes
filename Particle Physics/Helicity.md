Helicity is defined as the component of a particle's spin along it's direction of motion. The helicity operator is essentially a projection of the momentum operator onto the [[Pauli Matricies]] (spin operator matrices in each direction):
$$
\hat{h} = \frac{\hat{S}\cdot\hat{p}}{|\hat{S}||\hat{p}|}
$$
which has the positive energy eigenstates
$$
u_\uparrow(p) = \sqrt{E+m}\begin{pmatrix} c \\ e^{i\phi}s \\ \dfrac{|\vec{p}|}{E+m}c \\[8pt] \dfrac{|\vec{p}|}{E+m}e^{i\phi}s \end{pmatrix}, \qquad u_\downarrow(p) = \sqrt{E+m}\begin{pmatrix} -s \\ e^{i\phi}c \\ \dfrac{|\vec{p}|}{E+m}s \\[8pt] \dfrac{-|\vec{p}|}{E+m}e^{i\phi}c \end{pmatrix}
$$
$$
 v_\uparrow(p) = \sqrt{E+m}\begin{pmatrix} \dfrac{|\vec{p}|}{E+m}s \\[8pt] \dfrac{-|\vec{p}|}{E+m}e^{i\phi}c \\[8pt] -s \\ e^{i\phi}c \end{pmatrix}, \qquad v_2(p) = \sqrt{E+m}\begin{pmatrix} \dfrac{|\vec{p}|}{E+m}c \\[8pt] \dfrac{|\vec{p}|}{E+m}e^{i\phi}s \\[8pt] c \\ e^{i\phi}s \end{pmatrix} 
$$
where $c = \cos{\frac{\theta}{2}}$  and $s = \sin{\frac{\theta}{2}}$ with $\theta$ and $\phi$ being the spherical coordinate angles of $\vec{p}$ from the $z$ axis. $u_\uparrow$ and $u_\downarrow$ are the spin parallel and anti-parallel particle states and $v_\uparrow$ and $v_\downarrow$ are the spin parallel and anti-parallel anti-particle states. These states form a basis for Dirac Spinors and span the solution space of the [[Dirac Equation]].

The up states are called righthanded states and the down states are called lefthanded states.
