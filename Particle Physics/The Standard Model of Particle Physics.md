The standard model provides interaction rules and characteristics for all subatomic particles. 

There are 12 fundamental particles, called [[Fermions]], which are divided into 2 classes, Leptons and Quarks. Leptons are integer charged particles and experience the electromagnetic force and the weak force (neutrinos are also leptons with charge 0 that only experience the weak force). Quarks are fractionally charged particles that experience the strong force, electromagnetic force, and the weak force.
